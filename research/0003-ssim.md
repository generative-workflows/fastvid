# 0003 — Structural Similarity (SSIM)

Source: Z. Wang, A. Bovik, H. Sheikh, E. Simoncelli, “Image Quality
Assessment: From Error Visibility to Structural Similarity,” IEEE
Transactions on Image Processing 13(4), 2004.

- Author page: https://ece.uwaterloo.ca/~z70wang/publications/ssim.html
- Paper: https://ece.uwaterloo.ca/~z70wang/publications/ssim.pdf

## Findings

Mean squared error alone does not model structural degradation well. SSIM
compares luminance, contrast, and structure locally and correlates better with
subjective quality on the authors' test set.

## Fastvid implications

The first dependency-free harness reports exactness, maximum error, MSE, and
PSNR. SSIM is required before judging transforms or chroma subsampling changes;
it is not yet implemented in EXP-0001.
